# Ember Garden Lookout visual review

Viewed final home.png, front.png, top.png, and right.png. Silhouette reads as a small open red pavilion tower: gray ground slab and raised square deck, four slender red posts below and above, black broad flat canopy. Front bay is open. Checkerboard roof tiles alternate with visible studs, an intentionally patterned finish rather than a solid flush roof. Roof overhang is modest and consistent, with no obvious missing or intersecting pieces in these views. The base and deck have visible black stitching plates; this is intentionally exposed structural detailing.

First geometric version used 2x2 pillars: the contact graph separated deck and roof into six groups because the broad posts had no registered connection to the chosen slab surfaces. Changed to slender 1x1 pillar stacks and rerendered. Final reports 1 optimistic component with complete connector metadata, but 22 confirmed groups, including roof contacts; automatic connector evidence is insufficient to prove every joint. Physical strength, real clutch fit, roof uplift resistance and accessible route to raised deck are not verified. Collision review warning concerns non-rectangular profiled large slabs/tiles and stud/socket bounds; no curated body-overlap errors. BOM matches LeoCAD 56/56 and exact allowed part/colour pairs pass.
